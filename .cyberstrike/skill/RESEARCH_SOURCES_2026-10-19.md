## Reuse rules
Prefer official specifications and vendor docs for factual claims. Use labs for mechanics and community writeups as hypotheses, not proof. Do not copy upstream skill files wholesale; check the exact license, attribution, referenced scripts, and dependencies. If licensing or provenance is unclear, keep the source reference-only. Never claim a source or the full internet has been exhaustively reviewed.
